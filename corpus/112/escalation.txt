--init-groups`), because:
  * curiosito's password is unknown (yescrypt hash in /etc/shadow, 0 candidates
    tested against it — UNTESTED, not "uncrackable"), and
  * WordPress credentials were never obtained (see credential-sweep.txt), and
  * there is no unauthenticated RCE on this image (see plugin-defect.txt).

So: the escalation is a real, reproduced, measured finding. The entry condition
is not. Both facts belong in the report, separately — merging them would tell a
reader that something was patched that was not reached.
